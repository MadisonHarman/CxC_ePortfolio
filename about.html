About

First-generation, four-point-two, and not done yet.

I'm from Denham Springs, Louisiana, and I'm the first in my family to attend college. I chose computer science because it let me build things, and research because it let me build things that matter to the place I'm from — a state where a hurricane season is a fact of life, not a headline.

I'm also a Digital Media Arts & Engineering minor, which is where the design side of this site — and of projects like Roomies — comes from, and an Honors Research minor, which led to my capstone thesis.
